A little help.

Day Holder should make life simpler. For account, reminder, Family or purchase help, email michellelaird17@gmail.com.

Include your app version, iPhone model, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help; remove private reminders, email addresses and payment information first. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, API keys or full payment details.

My reminder did not appear

Check the reminder time and notification permission in iPhone Settings. Review Focus, notification summaries, sound and time-zone settings. Personal reminders use your device; shared Family reminders also need account access, notification opt-in and network delivery. Notifications are not guaranteed for time-critical situations.

I cannot sign in

Check your account confirmation email and spam folder. Use Forgot password on the account screen, open the newest recovery link and set a new password. Expired or already-used links need to be requested again. Contact support if you cannot access your inbox.

I already subscribed

Sign into the original Day Holder account and the App Store account used to purchase, then select Restore purchases on the subscription screen. A Family invitation covers approved members while the owner has active Family access; members do not each need to purchase Family.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Day Holder. Cancel before the trial or paid period ends to stop renewal. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing. For Apple refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

Delete my account or request my data

In Day Holder, open You → Account → Delete account and read the confirmation. A Family owner's deletion removes their owned Family and its shared content. If you cannot sign in or want help with access, export, correction or deletion, email support. We may need to verify ownership; never send a password.

Family access and privacy

The owner controls joining, child approval, relevant creation permissions and removal. Only explicitly shared Family content is visible to members. Personal memories and private photos do not become shared simply because you joined. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
