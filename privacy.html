Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07 · Operated by Michelle A Laird

Day Holder helps you remember everyday things. This policy explains the information used by the app, account sync, and Family features. Contact michellelaird17@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Medication reminders and location features have additional disclosures in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. That policy is also available from the app’s Privacy screen.

Information you provide

  • Account: email, account identifier, profile or display name, and authentication information managed by our authentication provider.
  • Your memories: reminders, tasks, homework, checklists, notes, dates, recurrence settings, subscription reminders, and other content you save. You may choose to include sensitive information; only add what you need.
  • Photos: images you choose to attach. Account attachments use private cloud storage. We do not automatically upload your photo library.
  • Saved places: names and coordinates you save when location features are enabled. Saved places may sync with your account. Optional arrival/departure reminders use device location and may require background location permission. Day Holder does not provide a live Family location-sharing feature.
  • Family: memberships, display names, roles, invitations, approvals, shared items, assignments and completion history. Active members can see shared Family content; personal reminders and photos remain private to your account.

Device, purchase and operational information

For Family reminders you enable, we store a device push token with your account. Our backend sends Expo a generic notification and Family/item routing identifiers; it does not include the user-entered Family item title or contents. Expo relays that generic notification to Apple Push Notification service or the applicable device push service and processes the push token and delivery metadata. Personal reminders are scheduled locally where supported. Medication names and saved-place details are omitted from notification text; a generic notice asks you to open Day Holder to view them. Other reminder titles may appear in local notifications depending on your device notification settings.

Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases and subscription validation. We process app account IDs, product and entitlement IDs, transaction-related identifiers, subscription status, expiry dates and billing lifecycle events. We do not receive your full payment card details. Reminders you manually create about other subscriptions are separate from purchasing Day Holder.

Supabase provides authentication, database, private photo storage, and Edge Functions. It receives account identity, synced memories and settings, saved places, family records, push tokens, and service requests. RevenueCat receives an app-specific user identifier and purchase/entitlement lifecycle data; Apple handles payment details and store transactions. Expo receives the opted-in Family push data described above. Apple Maps or Google Maps may receive a destination when you choose to open directions. Your device’s system geocoder receives an address when you ask Day Holder to locate a saved place. Hosting and service providers may process network and operational data such as IP addresses, request times, device or app details and error information.

Calendar integration

With permission, Day Holder reads device calendars to show or import relevant events. Saved or imported details can become account-synced memories. The current calendar integration does not edit your source calendar.

Why information is used and who receives it

Information is used to run accounts, sync saved content, deliver reminders, support shared Family activity, validate paid access, answer support requests, and prevent misuse. Supabase provides authentication, database and private storage in the United States (US West for the current project), including account confirmation and recovery messages. RevenueCat provides subscription infrastructure; Apple provides store billing and iOS notifications; Expo relays Family push notifications. Apple Maps or Google Maps may receive a destination when you choose to open directions.

Family content is shared with members you invite or approve, not publicly listed. Removing a member prevents further server access, but cannot erase screenshots or copies they previously saved. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to address abuse or security incidents. The app has no public profile discovery, advertising SDK or targeted-ad feature. This website uses no advertising or analytics scripts.

Storage, retention and deletion

Without an account, memories are stored on your device. When signed in, personal content and saved places sync to private cloud records. Family content is stored in separate shared records. Data is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized service infrastructure processes it. Device security and your account credentials also protect access.

We keep account content while your account is active or until you delete it. We retain deletion markers as needed to keep removed memories from reappearing during sync. Limited purchase, security, and service records may be kept when needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Copies in service-provider backups and logs may remain for their normal retention cycles after active data is removed. We do not promise a fixed backup deletion deadline; contact support with a specific retention or deletion question.

Deleting a memory records a deletion marker so other devices do not restore it accidentally. Deleting a memory does not necessarily immediately purge every previously uploaded photo. Account deletion removes the account's private records and attachments from active services; deleting a Family owner's account also removes the Family they own. Some billing, security, legal or backup records may remain under the retention policy above. Previously issued image links and caches may remain usable until they expire; downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Subscription expiry does not delete memories or Family history. Paid actions are restricted while retained data remains accessible under the app's free or read-only access rules. Deleting an account does not cancel App Store billing.

Your choices

You can edit or delete saved content, disconnect integrations, adjust permissions in device settings, disable Family notifications, leave a Family, or delete your account under You → Account. The in-app export covers memories, lists, and saved places; it is not a complete account-data export. For access to or correction of other account information, a copy of health data, or another privacy request, contact michellelaird17@gmail.com with the request type. We will verify account ownership and respond under applicable law. You may withdraw sensitive-data consent in Privacy settings; withdrawal stops future collection/use for that feature, and you can separately delete previously saved content or your account. Do not email passwords or recovery codes.

Children and Family accounts

Independent accounts are for people age 13 or older. Family members ages 13–17 may join with approval from their parent or guardian and the Family owner. Day Holder does not support independent accounts for children under 13. Family-owner approval controls app access, but it does not verify parental identity or serve as a separate legal consent system.

The current child/member flow uses a separate account and email address. The Family owner controls invitations, approval, removal and relevant creation permissions. Approval is an app access control; it is not itself a verified parental-consent process. Children cannot access an adult's private reminders through Family membership. Contact support if an account was created contrary to the age policy.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and provide additional notice where required. Questions: michellelaird17@gmail.com. Service help is available on our Support page.
