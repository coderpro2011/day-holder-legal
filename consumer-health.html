Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-07 · Operated by Michelle A Laird

This supplemental policy explains Day Holder’s handling of consumer health data, including information that may be protected by health-data or sensitive-data laws. It supplements the Privacy Policy. Questions or requests: michellelaird17@gmail.com.

Health data we process and why

If you choose to create a medication reminder, Day Holder processes the medication name you enter, schedule and recurrence, reminder settings, and completion status. The name and routine may reveal or allow an inference about a health condition. We use it only to save, sync, and display the reminder and schedule the notice you requested. Medication names and schedules are omitted from notification text; the notice asks you to open Day Holder to view the reminder. Day Holder does not prescribe, recommend, or verify medications, doses, or treatment.

If you enter a place in an event or choose a location-based feature, Day Holder processes the address or coordinates you provide, device location you choose to save, and (only after you enable departure reminders and grant operating-system permission) the local location event needed to determine whether you left a saved place. Location is used to save event/place details, schedule the requested reminder, and provide directions you request. Day Holder does not maintain a location history or continuously send device coordinates to our servers.

Sources and processors

The source is information you enter or select, and location supplied by your device or the system geocoder when you request a location feature. On-device reminders and geofence checks run locally. For signed-in accounts, the medication reminder, saved-place details, and account content sync to Supabase for storage. Supabase is our database, authentication, private storage, and backend provider. A destination is sent to Apple Maps or Google Maps only when you request directions; an address is sent to the device’s system geocoder when you request place lookup. Apple handles App Store billing; RevenueCat processes account identifier and subscription entitlement information, not medication or location content. Expo’s remote push relay is used only for Family reminders and receives a push token, generic notification text, and Family/item routing identifiers; it does not receive the user-entered Family item title or contents. Medication and location details are also omitted from local notification text; those notifications use generic wording.

When you deliberately put content into a shared Family item, Family members permitted to view that item can see it. Personal reminders are private by default. Day Holder does not sell consumer health data and does not use it for targeted advertising. It does not send medication or saved-place content to advertising networks.

Consent and your choices

Before the first medication reminder or location-based event/place feature is used, the app asks for a separate affirmative choice describing the data and purpose. Your choice is recorded on the device with its timestamp and this policy version. You can withdraw it from You → Privacy. Withdrawing stops future use for that feature and removes personal medication reminders, event place details, and saved places from this account; it cannot remove text or copies you deliberately shared into a Family item. Contact us to request deletion or restriction of shared content. You can also revoke device location permission in system settings, disable departure reminders, or delete your account.

Request access to or correction of your health data, deletion, a copy, or information about sharing by emailing michellelaird17@gmail.com. If we deny a request, reply to our response with “Appeal” and the reason you believe it should be reconsidered. The app’s on-device export is limited to memories, lists, and saved places and is not a complete account export. We verify requests and respond within the time required by applicable law. We do not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Retention and security

Health-related content is kept while your account remains active or until you delete it. Active account deletion removes the account’s active private records and attachments. Deletion markers, provider backups, and limited operational records may remain for their usual retention cycles or where needed for legal, security, or billing purposes, as described in the Privacy Policy. Access is restricted through authenticated accounts and private storage policies; data is not end-to-end encrypted.

Legal scope

Some state laws define consumer health data broadly, and their scope and exemptions depend on the operator, consumer, data, and transaction. This policy is intended to give a clear disclosure and choice without representing that every health privacy statute applies to every user or that Day Holder is covered by HIPAA. Applicable rights may vary by location.
